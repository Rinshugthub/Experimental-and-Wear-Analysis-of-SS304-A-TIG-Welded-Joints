# Experimental and Wear Analysis of SS304 TIG Welded Joints

## Project Overview
This project investigates the experimental and wear performance of SS304 stainless steel TIG welded joints. The study evaluates the effect of TIG welding on the wear characteristics and overall performance of the welded specimens through experimental testing and analysis.

## Objectives
- Study the TIG welding process for SS304 stainless steel.
- Prepare and test welded specimens.
- Analyze the wear behavior of the welded joints.
- Compare experimental results and draw conclusions.

## Methodology
1. Preparation of SS304 specimens.
2. TIG welding of the specimens.
3. Wear testing of the welded joints.
4. Collection and analysis of experimental data.
5. Interpretation of results.

## Tools and Equipment
- TIG Welding Machine
- SS304 Stainless Steel Specimens
- Wear Testing Machine
- Measuring Instruments
- Microsoft Excel (Data Analysis)

## Key Outcomes
- Evaluated the wear performance of SS304 TIG welded joints.
- Analyzed experimental data to compare wear characteristics.
- Identified factors affecting the performance of welded joints.
